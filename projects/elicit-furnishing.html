05 — Elicit Furnishing

A production web app replacing pen-and-paper sales tracking — built for a real furniture retail client who uses it every day.

Client Elicit Furnishing
Role Designer & Developer
Year 2025
Type Full-Stack · Supabase · PWA
Live Open the App →

The Problem

74 products tracked by hand across 8 categories — no history, no reports

Every sale on paper. No inventory visibility. No way to know what sold last week.

What Was Built

Five systems. One app. Zero training required.

  • Sales logging with searchable product picker across all 74 items
  • Real-time revenue dashboard with 7-day chart and transaction history
  • Automated weekly and monthly email reports — delivered without the client doing anything

Outcome

Live. Used daily. Pen and paper gone.
