01 — QuickFit

An AI-powered parts intelligence platform that eliminates the supply run for GTA tradespeople.

Role Designer & Developer
Year 2025
Type AI · Product · Web
Live Open the App →

The Problem

90 min lost per job to supply runs

The parts exist. There's no intelligence layer between the site and the supplier.

What Was Built

Describe the job. Get the parts. They arrive in 30 minutes.

  • Job Estimator — plain language to a parts list with quantities and costs
  • Supply Chain Analyzer — cheapest path across live GTA supplier inventory
  • Runner delivers to site. The contractor never left.

Outcome

The intelligence layer is done. What’s left isn’t a technical problem — it’s a supplier partnership.

  • Both AI tools work end-to-end — the Job Estimator and Supply Chain Analyzer handle natural language input, call Claude, and return structured, accurate outputs
  • The only gap is live inventory data — no public API exists for GTA hardware supplier pricing; the moment a supplier opens one, this product is ready
  • Full product thinking, solo — research, branding, design, AI integration, and deployment; and honest communication about what a real product would need next
  • Claude API
