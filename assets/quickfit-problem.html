Before

What contractors saw

No visible product inventory

Contractors couldn't confirm the supplier stocked what they needed without calling or visiting in person.

No trust indicators or credentials

Generic layout, no business history, no supplier affiliations — nothing to signal legitimacy in 10 seconds.

Contact buried below the fold

Phone number and quote request required scrolling past content most trade visitors would never reach.

Generic copy, no trade-specific language

Copy read like a template. No reference to contractors, job sites, trade accounts, or GTA delivery.

After

What the redesign answered

Products surfaced in the first scroll

Category navigation and AI-generated product descriptions visible before the contractor decides to leave.

Credibility signals above the fold

Trade-specific headline, supplier affiliations, and brand identity communicate permanence immediately.

Quote CTA visible within 3 seconds

Persistent nav CTA and above-fold contact path. No scrolling required to take the next step.

AI-generated trade-specific product copy

Claude API generates category and product copy in the contractor's language — specifications, quantities, trade context.

The Brief

Build something that converts. Trade buyers don't browse — they scan for legitimacy, inventory, and contact. The site had 10 seconds to answer all three.
